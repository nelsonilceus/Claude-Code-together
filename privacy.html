What we collect

  • Early access form: first name, email address, ZIP code, and whether you want launch updates.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect payment details, dental records, insurance information, or health information on this site. Please don’t include health information in the message field.

How we use it

To understand interest in NOVA Dental by area, to contact you about launch availability and participating practices, and to follow up with dental practices about the founding pilot. We don’t sell your information.
